# Small manual qualitative review

Reviewed 2026-10-22 by a separate LLM reviewer from the draft author. This was one manual, unblinded pass through six synthetic prompts. The reviewer saw the skill and its illustrative examples before answering. It was not an automated benchmark, controlled comparison, human evaluation, or measurement of improvement. No model training or repeated sampling was performed.

## Rewrites and meaning checks

### A. Comparison

I'll compare the options.

The commitment to compare remains. The context gives no reason to rule out a useful recommendation. Removing the contrast neither promises a recommendation nor restricts the comparison.

### B. Limited result

The trial reduced latency in this sample, but it did not establish a production reliability improvement.

Unchanged. The sample-specific finding and the lack of established production reliability improvement are both necessary. Absence of evidence of an improvement is not evidence that improvement is impossible.

### C. Blocker and offer

I can prepare the upload. The service is offline, so I can't upload the file yet.

Unchanged. Preparation remains an offer or capability, not completed work or a promise to act. The current inability to upload and its stated cause remain explicit; no recovery time is invented.

### D. Uncertainty and approval

The outage may be related to the deploy. I'll inspect the logs on Friday at 14:00 UTC. I won't roll back without approval.

The possible cause stays uncertain. The scheduled inspection, day, time, time zone, and approval requirement remain. This does not promise a rollback or imply that approval has already been given.

### E. Exact text

The label reads ‘Do not disconnect.’ The check is `if not connected: stop()`. These details are not optional.

Only the introductory ceremony was removed. The quotation, code, and all three meaningful negations remain exactly as supplied. The statement that the details are mandatory remains.

### F. Accuracy correction

The detector flagged this paragraph, but that does not prove it was written by AI.

The reported detector flag remains. The unsupported claim of proof is corrected rather than preserved as fact. This is an accuracy correction, not merely a stylistic edit; it makes no claim about who actually wrote the paragraph.

## Assessment and limits

For these six outputs, the reviewer found the relevant facts, uncertainty, commitments, conditions, and exact quoted/code content preserved, with the explicit accuracy correction in F. No output adds a deadline, permission, capability, completed action, or promise. B and C show that an already clear sentence can stay unchanged. These judgments are inspectable above, not a claim that future outputs will reliably behave the same way. Broader claims would need held-out cases, repeated runs, independent blinded judgments, and reader-oriented measures.
